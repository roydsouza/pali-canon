### Linked Discourses 35.85
*Saṁyutta Nikāya 35.85*

### 9. With Channa
*9. Channavagga*

### The World is Empty
*Suññatalokasutta*
> **Pāli:** Atha kho āyasmā ānando …pe… bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
> **English:** And then Venerable Ānanda … said to the Buddha:
>
> **Pāli:** “‘suñño loko, suñño loko’ti, bhante, vuccati.
> **English:** “Sir, they say that ‘the world is empty’.
>
> **Pāli:** Kittāvatā nu kho, bhante, suñño lokoti vuccatī”ti?
> **English:** What does the saying ‘the world is empty’ refer to?”
>
> **Pāli:** “Yasmā ca kho, ānanda, suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā tasmā suñño lokoti vuccati.
> **English:** “Ānanda, they say that ‘the world is empty’ because it’s empty of self or what belongs to self.
>
> **Pāli:** Kiñca, ānanda, suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā?
> **English:** And what is empty of self or what belongs to self?
>
> **Pāli:** Cakkhu kho, ānanda, suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā.
> **English:** The eye,
>
> **Pāli:** Rūpā suññā attena vā attaniyena vā, cakkhuviññāṇaṁ suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā, cakkhusamphasso suñño attena vā attaniyena vā …pe…
> **English:** sights, eye consciousness, and eye contact are empty of self or what belongs to self. …
>
> **Pāli:** yampidaṁ manosamphassapaccayā uppajjati vedayitaṁ sukhaṁ vā dukkhaṁ vā adukkhamasukhaṁ vā tampi suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā.
> **English:** The pleasant, painful, or neutral feeling that arises dependent on mind contact is also empty of self or what belongs to self.
>
> **Pāli:** Yasmā ca kho, ānanda, suññaṁ attena vā attaniyena vā, tasmā suñño lokoti vuccatī”ti.
> **English:** They say that ‘the world is empty’ because it’s empty of self or what belongs to self.”
>
> **Pāli:** Dutiyaṁ.
> **English:**
