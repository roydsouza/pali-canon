# Pericope Concordance (Vākya-saṅgaha)
**Navigation**: [[Texts-Index|Pali Canon Vault]]

This concordance maps the recurrent stock formulas (*pericopes*) that repeat across hundreds of suttas. Recognising these formulas dramatically accelerates Pali reading: once the formula is memorised, only the variable parts need to be decoded.

---

## 🧘 Meditative Attainments

### 1. The Four Jhānas Formula
*   **Setting**: Meditation attainment, gradual training sequences.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati..."*
*   **Description**:
    *   **First Jhāna**: Accompanied by directed thought and evaluation, and the rapture and pleasure born of seclusion.
    *   **Second Jhāna**: Free from directed thought and evaluation, with internal confidence and unification of mind, and the rapture and pleasure born of concentration.
    *   **Third Jhāna**: With the fading of rapture, remaining equanimous, mindful, and alert, experiencing pleasure with the body.
    *   **Fourth Jhāna**: Neither pleasure nor pain, with the purification of mindfulness by equanimity.
*   **Loci**:
    *   [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn118|MN 118]] · [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2]] · [[matika/four_jhanas|Mātikā: Four Jhānas]]

### 2. The Three Formless Attainments Formula
*   **Setting**: Continuation of jhāna sequence beyond the four material jhānas.
*   **Pali Text** (Ākāsānañcāyatana):
    > *"Sabbaso rūpasaññānaṃ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṃ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṃ amanasikārā 'ananto ākāso'ti ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharati."*
*   **Description**: Transcending form entirely, the practitioner enters the formless realms: infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness, and the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn36|MN 36]] · [[pakarana/visuddhimagga/vsm10_arupajhana|Vsm Ch. X]]

### 3. The End of Taints Formula (*Āsavakkhaya*)
*   **Setting**: Culmination of the gradual training; declaration of arahantship.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"So evaṃ samāhite citte ... āsavānaṃ khayañāṇāya cittaṃ abhininnāmeti. So 'idaṃ dukkhan'ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti..."*
    > *"Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ, kataṃ karaṇīyaṃ, nāparaṃ itthattāyāti pajānāti."*
    > *("Birth is destroyed, the holy life has been lived, what had to be done has been done, there is no more coming to any state of being.")*
*   **Setting detail**: The concentrated mind is directed toward knowledge of the destruction of the taints, knowing the Four Noble Truths directly, which destroys the defilements.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn36|MN 36]] · [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/theragatha/Mula-Theragatha-Index|Theragāthā (all arhant verses)]]

## ⛓️ Doctrinal Chains

### 1. The 12-Linked Dependent Origination Chain
*   **Setting**: Analytical description of the origination of suffering.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā; saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ; viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ; nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ; saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso; phassapaccayā vedanā; vedanāpaccayā taṇhā; taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ; upādānapaccayā bhavo; bhavapaccayā jāti; jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ..."*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn15|DN 15]] · [[mula/sutta/samyutta_nikaya/sn12|SN 12: Nidānasaṃyutta]] · [[matika/dependent_origination|Mātikā: Dependent Origination]]

### 2. The Cessation Sequence (*Nirodha-vāra*)
*   **Setting**: Immediately follows the origination sequence as its mirror.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Avijjāya tveva asesavirāganirodhā saṅkhāranirodho; saṅkhāranirodhā viññāṇanirodho..."*
    > *("But with the fading away and cessation of ignorance without remainder, formations cease; with the cessation of formations, consciousness ceases...")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/samyutta_nikaya/sn12|SN 12: Nidānasaṃyutta]] · [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn15|DN 15]]

## 🧩 Insight & Contemplation

### 1. The Satipaṭṭhāna Refrain (*Anupassanā-vāra*)
*   **Setting**: Repeated after each contemplation section in MN 10 / DN 22.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Iti ajjhattaṃ vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati, bahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati, ajjhattabahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati. Samudayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati, vayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati, samudayavayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati..."*
    > *("Thus he dwells observing the body in the body internally... externally... internally and externally. He dwells observing the origination... the dissolution... the origination and dissolution of phenomena in the body...")*
*   **Meaning**: The refrain establishes three spatial perspectives (internal/external/both), three temporal perspectives (arising/passing/both), bare attention (*natthi ṭhānaṃ*) without clinging.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10: Satipaṭṭhānasutta]] · [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn22|DN 22: Mahāsatipaṭṭhānasutta]]

### 2. Aggregates Liberation Formula (*Anattalakkhaṇa*)
*   **Setting**: Discourses on insight into the three marks.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"N'etaṃ mama, n'esohamasmi, na meso attā."*
    > *("This is not mine, this I am not, this is not my self.")*
*   **Meaning**: Applied systematically to each of the five aggregates to dismantle the illusion of identity.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn28|MN 28]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn44|MN 44]] · [[matika/five_aggregates|Mātikā: Five Aggregates]]

### 3. The Arising and Passing Formula (*Samudaya-vaya*)
*   **Setting**: Insight knowledge that marks a breakthrough in vipassanā practice.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Samudayadhammā, vayadhammā, samudayavayadhammā"*
    > *("Of the nature to arise, of the nature to pass, of the nature to both arise and pass.")*
*   **Meaning**: Direct perception of impermanence in moment-to-moment experience. Repeated as the practitioner's recognition that all conditioned phenomena are governed by this law.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10 §§]] · [[mula/sutta/samyutta_nikaya/sn22|SN 22]] · [[matika/impermanence|Mātikā: Impermanence]]

### 4. Element Dissection Formula
*   **Setting**: Mindfulness of body element analysis.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Kāyaṃyeva yathābhūtaṃ yathāṭhitaṃ dhātuso paccavekkhati: 'Atthi imasmiṃ kāye pathavīdhātu āpodhātu tejodhātu vāyodhātū'ti."*
    > *("One reviews this very body, however it stands or is positioned, by way of elements: 'In this body there is the earth element, the water element, the fire element, the wind element.'")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10 §4.1]] · [[matika/contemplation_of_body|Mātikā: Contemplation of the Body]]

## 🛤️ The Gradual Training (*Anupubbasikkhā*)

### 1. Full Gradual Training Pericope
*   **Setting**: The standard account of how a new disciple progresses from hearing the Dhamma to liberation. Appears in condensed or expanded form in scores of suttas.
*   **Pali Text (opening)**:
    > *"Tathāgato loke uppajjati arahaṃ sammāsambuddho... so tassa dhammaṃ deseti... sāvako... saddhājāto... agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajati..."*
    > *("A Tathāgata arises in the world... he teaches the Dhamma... a follower, with faith arisen, goes forth from the home into homelessness...")*
*   **Sequence**: Goes forth → guards the sense doors → mindful and alert → content → abandons the five hindrances → enters the four jhānas → gains supernormal knowledges → destroys the taints.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2: Sāmaññaphalasutta]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn27|MN 27]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn51|MN 51]] · [[matika/gradual_training|Mātikā: Gradual Training]]

### 2. Sense Restraint Formula (*Indriyasaṃvara*)
*   **Setting**: Step three of the gradual training; often appears standalone.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā na nimittaggāhī hoti nānuvyañjanaggāhī..."*
    > *("Having seen a form with the eye, one does not grasp at a sign, does not grasp at features...")*
*   **Meaning**: Not grasping the *nimitta* (salient feature that triggers association and reactivity) is the key. The formula is repeated for all six sense bases.
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn27|MN 27]] · [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2]] · [[matika/contemplation_of_mind|Mātikā: Contemplation of Mind]]

### 3. The Five Hindrances Abandonment Formula
*   **Setting**: Just before jhāna entry in the gradual training; describes the abandoning of each hindrance.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"So ime pañca nīvaraṇe pahāya... kāmacchanda... byāpāda... thinamiddha... uddhaccakukkucca... vicikiccha... cittassa upakkilese paññāya dubbalīkaraṇe pahāya..."*
    > *("Having abandoned these five hindrances, corruptions of the mind that weaken wisdom...")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/digha_nikaya/dn2|DN 2]] · [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10]] · [[matika/five_hindrances|Mātikā: Five Hindrances]]

## 🙏 Recollection & Devotion

### 1. The Triple Recollection Formula (*Anussati-vāra*)
*   **Setting**: Recollection of the Buddha, Dhamma, and Saṅgha. Appears in AN 6 and MN texts.
*   **Pali Text** (Buddhānussati):
    > *"Iti pi so bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṃ buddho bhagavā'ti."*
    > *("Indeed the Blessed One is such — an arahant, fully awakened, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, fortunate, knower of the world, unsurpassed trainer of persons to be tamed, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/anguttara_nikaya/an6_10|AN 6.10]] · [[matika/six_recollections|Mātikā: Six Recollections]] · [[matika/buddha|Mātikā: Buddha]]

### 2. The Loving-Kindness Radiation Formula (*Mettā-vāra*)
*   **Setting**: Standard mettā meditation instruction and recitation.
*   **Pali Text** (abbreviated):
    > *"Mettāsahagatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati, tathā dutiyaṃ, tathā tatiyaṃ, tathā catutthaṃ; iti uddhamadho tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ lokaṃ mettāsahagatena cetasā vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjhena pharitvā viharati."*
    > *("One dwells having suffused one direction with a mind of loving-kindness, likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth; above, below, across, everywhere, to all as to oneself, the entire world with a mind of loving-kindness — vast, great, boundless, without enmity, without ill will.")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/majjhima_nikaya/mn10|MN 10]] · [[mula/sutta/anguttara_nikaya/an11_15|AN 11.15]] · [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/sutta_nipata/snp1_8|Snp 1.8]]

## 🏆 Spiritual Fruits

### 1. The Declaration of Arahanthood
*   **Setting**: The standard ending of a disciple's discourse when they attain liberation.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ, kataṃ karaṇīyaṃ, nāparaṃ itthattāyāti pajānātīti."*
    > *("Birth is destroyed, the holy life has been lived, what had to be done has been done, there is no more coming to any state of being.")*
*   **Loci**: [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/theragatha/Mula-Theragatha-Index|Theragāthā: All Arhant verses]] · [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/therigatha/Mula-Therigatha-Index|Therīgāthā: All Arhant verses]] · [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/theragatha/thag1_1|THAG 1.1]] · [[mula/sutta/khuddaka_nikaya/therigatha/thig1_1|THIG 1.1]]

### 2. Post-Discourse Closing Formula (*Idamavoca-vāra*)
*   **Setting**: Closes virtually every sutta in the four main nikāyas.
*   **Pali Text**:
    > *"Idamavoca bhagavā. Attamanā ... bhagavato bhāsitaṃ abhinandunti."*
    > *("Thus spoke the Blessed One. Delighted, the ... rejoiced in the Blessed One's words.")*
*   **Meaning**: More than a literary convention — indicates the assembly's gladness and assent, and functions as the formal closing of a teaching event.
*   **Note**: The gap ("...") fills with the name of the audience — monks, a lay person, a deva, etc. Recognising this formula immediately locates the end of a discourse in the Pali text.
