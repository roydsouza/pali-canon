# Purification of Virtue (Sīla-visuddhi)

**Navigation**: [[../Texts-Index|Pali Canon Vault]] / [[Matika-Index|Mātika]] / [[seven_purifications|Seven Purifications]]

---

## Canonical Definition

> "Sīlavisuddhi kho pana... pātimokkhasaṃvarasīlaṃ..."
> 
> *“Purification of virtue is... moral discipline according to monastic code...”*

---

## Detailed Description

The first purification. It is the refinement of moral behavior, restraint of the senses, and pure livelihood. It establishes the clean foundation upon which meditation can succeed.
