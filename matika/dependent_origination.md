## Canonical References
* [[dn15|DN 15: Mahānidāna Sutta]] (The locus classicus for the detailed analysis and mutual conditioning of name-and-form and consciousness)
* [[mn118|MN 118: Ānāpānasati Sutta]] (referenced implicitly in the section on contemplation of feeling and mind, and commentarial discussions on the cessation of suffering)
* [[mn43|MN 43: Mahāvedallasutta]] — avijjā and taṇhā identified as the two bonds (saṃyojana) that keep beings roaming in saṃsāra; cessation of avijjā and taṇhā leads to liberation; the sutta's discussion of feeling and consciousness touches the central links of the chain
* [[dhp_24_tanhavagga|Dhp Ch. 24: Taṇhāvagga (Craving)]] — the entire chapter is a poetic treatment of taṇhā (link 7 in the chain) as the engine of continued existence; v. 338-339 use the tree-root simile to show why craving must be uprooted entirely, not merely cut back
* [[sn35|SN 35: Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta]] — the six sense bases (saḷāyatana = link 4) are the pivot of the chain; SN 35 consistently shows how contact (phassa = link 5) and feeling (vedanā = link 6) arising through the āyatanā drive taṇhā; the Fire Sermon (sn35.28) teaches dispassion toward the burning āyatanā as the way to break the chain
* [[sn12|SN 12: Nidānasaṃyutta]] — the primary canonical locus for dependent origination; SN 12.1-2 state the standard 12-link formula and its analysis; SN 12.15 (Kaccānagotta) presents DO as the middle-way teaching between existence and non-existence; SN 12.23 (Upanisā) extends the chain upward from saddhā to liberation; SN 12.65 (Nagarasūpamā) narrates the Buddha's rediscovery of the ancient path through following the chain to cessation
* [[udana#Ud 1.1: Paṭhamabodhisutta — *Upon Awakening (1st)*|Ud 1.1, 1.2, 1.3: Bodhi Suttas]] — The Buddha's contemplation of dependent origination in forward (*anuloma*), reverse (*paṭiloma*), and both orders immediately following his awakening at the foot of the Bodhi tree.
* [[dn1|DN 1: Brahmajālasutta]] — the 62 views arise through the dependent sequence of contact → feeling → craving → clinging → view; the sutta's analysis of how speculative views arise implicitly maps onto the paṭiccasamuppāda chain.
* [[dn16|DN 16: Mahāparinibbānasutta]] — the Buddha's final teachings include dependent origination as a central doctrinal summary; the mirror of Dhamma passage teaches stream-entry through understanding conditionality.
