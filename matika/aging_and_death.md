# Aging and Death (Jarāmaraṇa)

**Navigation**: [[../Texts-Index|Pali Canon Vault]] / [[Matika-Index|Mātika]] / [[dependent_origination|Dependent Origination]]

---

## Canonical Definition

> "Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ jarā jīrānataṃ... yaṃ tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ maraṇaṃ..."
> 
> *“The decay, decrepitude, brokenness... the passing away, dissolution, disappearance of various beings...”*

---

## Detailed Description

Jarāmaraṇa is the inevitable decay, decrepitude, and final dissolution of the psycho-physical organism. It represents the culmination of birth, followed by grief, lamentation, pain, distress, and despair.
